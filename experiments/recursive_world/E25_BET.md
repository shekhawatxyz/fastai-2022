# E25 research bet — selective transfer across real time, not same-event retries
2026-11-25. Proposed, not executed. Stop numerical boat tuning. Previous work preserved.
Target: CWM official Table 17 (https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24778v1), three-step replanning, RMS standardized distance < .2, 300 episodes per cell across three predictor seeds, two encoders and four environments. No-disturbance macro mean across eight cells: ordinary predictor 46.875%, unconditional residual reuse 35.25%, opportunity 11.625 pp. Persistent and fresh cases must be retained. Neither method is a verified world record. CWM already recognizes the persistence limitation and studies longer histories; do not claim discovery.

Panini A1.1.56-59: selective inheritance, not copying indiscriminately, as motivation only.
Design: fixed upstream CWM encoder/predictor/readout/CEM. Replace constant addition of last residual across consecutive events with a regularized Bayesian state estimator of persistent actuator perturbation in the frozen model's action coordinates, plus a separately estimated persistent output-space residual. For commanded action a use f(z,T_theta(a)) + b, where T is a low-dimensional gain/offset/rotation family as applicable, b output bias. Infer theta,b jointly from earlier factual transitions, nominal error covariance and prior shrinkage; linearized observation J_t theta + b, with J_t derived by autodiff/finite differences of frozen predictor, not handwritten physics. Use low-rank/regularized information updates, no future, same data. Fresh innovation enters noise, not the next-step correction. If few transitions cannot distinguish actuator shift from output drift, marginalize rather than hard-select; retain theta=b=0 prior. This is established Bayesian identification/filtering, not a novelty claim. ACTUS + bias is not automatically superior; overlapping J and b can be unidentifiable and model errors correlated. Same-event one-shot branch unchanged. Compare frozen, unconditional last residual, calibrated scalar shrinkage, output-bias Kalman, ACTUS alone, joint estimator (and upstream history model when available). Same calibration budget and protocol; no per-condition labels or hidden simulator state at inference. Calibration using disjoint nominal/factual shift episodes must be equally available, report information/compute costs. Actual ACTUS checkpoint/task results not interchangeable with CWM.
