Read Schmidhuber's Adaptive Confidence and Adaptive Curiosity (1991, https://people.idsia.ch/~juergen/FKI-149-91ocr.pdf), Curious Model-Building Control Systems (1991, https://people.idsia.ch/~juergen/curioussingapore/curioussingapore.html), History Compression (1992, doi:10.1162/NECO.1992.4.2.234), and Fast-Weight Memories (1992, doi:10.1162/NECO.1992.4.1.131). The useful principle is modeling learnable prediction errors and confidence separately from the slow predictor, not imposing recursion. In CWM (https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24778v1), Appendix C's same-event retry uses the previous attempt's readout residual. Test retaining the observed residual history with ordinary GP regression. Patañjali generality/exception is motivation, not a claimed novel algorithm.
