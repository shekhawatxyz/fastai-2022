## Engineering extrapolation
Do not add another fixed latent feature bank, recurrence layer, or symbolic rule. Jointly learn a representation and the transformations that it supports. A concept is an overlapping, operation-dependent equivalence relation, not an immutable category: two contexts may support transfer for one intervention but not another. Learn selective inheritance under changes, retain latent history only when it changes predictions, and let evidence from newly learned operations modify previous equivalences. This is related to causal abstraction, bisimulation, equivariance and learned modular dynamics; a novelty claim requires comparison with these. It is not an assertion that Pāṇini specified ML or that these rules alone guarantee SOTA.

For counterfactual dynamics, the important missing contract is how to determine that an inferred response function transfers to a different action/state/context. E19 and E20 show that neither a learned basis nor probabilistic context grouping alone beats strong generic regression reliably. Learn action-conditional transfer from factual probes on source environments; validate on independent interventions, with matching context, state and compute. Avoid hidden counterfactual supervision unless supplied equally to all arms and declared. Compare operation-conditioned representation learning with ordinary neural processes, deep kernels, causal representation learning and simple regression. Include negative transfer, action-effect error and decision regret, not only overall R².
