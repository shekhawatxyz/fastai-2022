# E16 — A learned event becomes a unit of later event induction
Frozen before execution. E1–E15 preserved. Textual impetus: Bhartṛhari VP 2.143–144 (whole understanding distinct from constituent meanings), 2.314 (meaning depends on context), 3.7.133 (whole vs parts of action), and Kaiyaṭa/Puṇyarāja on expectancy and semantic fit, see H. Ogawa, Approaching the sentence meaning in the Mahābhāṣya. Not an equivalence to an ancient algorithm.
Four unlabeled actions A,B,C,D transform a scene of 3 or 4 slots by hidden permutations. A,B random permutations; C is a word of length 1–3 in A,B; D is a word of length 1–2 in A,B,C. The learner enumerates words, not named effects. Source pretraining 96 worlds, full action truth via a distinct-symbol input, latent word identities hidden; learn fractional frequencies of all consistent word pairs. Source and target arities differ (3→4 and 4→3). Matched world templates (AB,CA) and (BA,BC); shifted templates swap D pairings; independent null samples C,D as arbitrary permutations. Templates are intentionally structured positive controls, not independent natural domains.
Each test world: one repeated-symbol input/output per A,B,C; zero, one, two or four D observations. Independent random binary inputs per observation; no noise in this first identification test. Test all binary scenes exactly, but exclude any observed input for that action. Same data for every arm. 80 worlds per family per direction, seeds 16161 and 16262. Source full observation counts and hypothesis enumeration charged. Evaluate exact next-state accuracy; also exact identification of latent permutations. Comparators: independent exact permutation posterior, exact joint hierarchical uniform prior, factorized learned word prior (same marginals), joint learned prior, robust mixture of independent and joint learned models (equal prior odds). Freeze C posterior at zero D observations to measure backward reinterpretation. Shared and independent evidence computed under normalized priors. All models may use the same full history; this is standard Bayesian structural induction, not a novelty claim.
Predictions before run: at zero D exposures learned joint vs uniform +5 pp exact held-out scenes on matched tasks; vs factorized +2 pp; at 2 D exposures backward C vs frozen +2 pp; robust vs independent on independent worlds at 2 exposures >=-2 pp. Gate: each transfer direction meets +3 pp vs uniform, +1 pp vs factorized and +1 pp backward; null harm <=2 pp; paired world bootstrap intervals exclude zero on matched forward/backward gains. Report shifted harm too. No GPU gate: even passing does not beat a real SOTA. A joint exact baseline with same prior is the same algorithm. This tests whether learned dependencies over event compositions are valuable, not whether recursion alone improves learning.
